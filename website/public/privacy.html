Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026.

El Paradiso Terminal does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The app makes HTTPS requests to elp.elparadisogonzalo.net only to download the bootstrap rootfs, package indexes, and package binaries you explicitly request. No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs.

Crash logs remain on-device unless you attach them to a bug report.
